        Frequently asked questions

        Do I need the original box or manual?

        No. A clear photo of the label is enough for this flow.

        Who can see my passport?

        Only you, on this device. Sharing with a technician is planned as an owner-controlled link.

        What if the AI gets the model wrong?

        When confidence is below 80%, you pick the correct model from a list before the passport is created.

        How does the recurring-fault flag work?

        If the same part is logged twice within 365 days, the passport shows a recurring flag for that part.

        Which appliances are covered?

        Home appliances: laundry, kitchen, cooling, climate and TVs. Computers and phones are outside this product.
